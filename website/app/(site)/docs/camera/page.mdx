# Camera overlay

Turn on the camera in the recording picker to add a bubble with your face on top of the screen recording. The bubble is drawn into the video itself, so it appears in the MP4 and in every share link.

## Place it

- **Drag** the bubble over the source preview to move it. It snaps to the corners with a small margin.
- **Drag the handle** to resize it, or use the **S / M / L** presets.
- The bubble always stays fully inside the frame.

## Style it

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Circle, rounded square, square |
| Size | 10% to 45% of the video's short side |
| Mirror | On (like a mirror, the default) or off |
| Border | None, subtle or accent |

Your choices are saved and reused for the next recording.

## While recording

From the floating control bar you can toggle the camera on and off, switch size (S/M/L), and snap the bubble to another corner without stopping.

## Tips

- Grant **Camera** access in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera** if the preview stays black.
- Close other apps that are using the camera (video calls, Photo Booth) if Capto can't open it.
- For talking-head messages, a larger size in a corner away from important UI works best.
