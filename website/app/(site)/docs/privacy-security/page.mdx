# Privacy & security

Capto is local-first. There is no Capto account, no Capto server and no analytics in the app.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Recordings** are written to `~/Movies/Capto/`.
- **Settings** (provider, region, bucket, prefix, default expiry) live in `~/Library/Application Support/capto/settings.json`. No secrets are stored there.
- **Credentials** (access keys, account keys, service-account JSON) are encrypted with Electron's `safeStorage`, whose encryption key is protected by the **macOS Keychain**, and saved to `~/Library/Application Support/capto/credentials.bin`.

After you save credentials, the settings screen only shows a hint like `…AB12`. The app's UI process never receives the secret; uploads and signing happen in Capto's main process.

## What leaves your Mac

Only what you choose to share, and only to **your** provider:

- The video file, poster image and (if present) captions, uploaded to your bucket over HTTPS.
- Signing a link happens locally. No network request is needed to create it.

Capto doesn't contact any Capto-operated service. Credentials are never sent anywhere except to your storage provider's API as part of signed requests.

## The viewer page

Viewer links look like `https://capto.ljappsolutions.com/v#…`. Everything after `#` stays in the viewer's browser: it is never sent to the website's server. The page decodes it locally and plays the video straight from your bucket. The viewer page:

- has no analytics, cookies or tracking scripts;
- sends no referrer (`Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`), so your bucket's logs don't learn the viewer page URL;
- is excluded from search engines.

Anyone who has the link can watch the video until it expires, so share it like you'd share a private document.

## Good practice

- Use a **dedicated bucket or prefix** and **least-privilege credentials** (each setup guide shows how).
- Keep the bucket **private**. Capto never needs public access.
- Use short expiry for sensitive videos, and **Delete from cloud** when you're done.
- Add a **lifecycle rule** to auto-delete old shares.

## Removing everything

- **Settings → Sharing → Remove credentials** wipes `credentials.bin`.
- Delete `~/Library/Application Support/capto/` to remove all settings.
- Delete `~/Movies/Capto/` to remove recordings.
